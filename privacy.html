Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

PyKnowledge ("we", "our") is an offline-first Python learning platform operated by Kerr Fairtex for TRAC students in Bongao, Tawi-Tawi, Philippines. This policy explains what information the app handles and why — in plain terms.

Short version

All of your data stays on your device. PyKnowledge has no server-side accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is ever transmitted to us or to any third party.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally using your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage:

  • Local profile — a display name and password you create. The password is stored only as a local credential check; it is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Learning progress — completed lessons, quiz scores, achievements, and unlocked modules.
  • Preferences — settings needed to run the app offline.

This data exists only on the device you use. Deleting the app or its site data permanently removes it.

What we do NOT collect

  • No personal information transmitted to any server
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No cookies used for tracking (storage is functional only)

Permissions

The app requests no special device permissions. As an installable web app / Android app, it uses storage only to cache lesson content and save your progress so everything works without an internet connection.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Kerr Fairtex at kerrfairtex@gmail.com.

Changes

If our data practices ever change (for example, if optional cloud sync is added), we will update this page and revise the date above before the change takes effect.

Built for real conditions

Made for shared computers and patchy signal

Every design choice here starts from the same constraint: this has to work on modest hardware, without a steady connection.

Install once, use anywhere

Add PyKnowledge to your home screen. After that first load, lessons, exercises, and quizzes all run with no connection.

Your progress stays with you

Set a PIN on a shared computer and your progress, scores, and unlocked modules stay separate from the next student.

Real code, real feedback

Write actual Python in the browser and get told exactly what's right, what's off, and why — no waiting on a grader.

About

A free Python course that works where the signal doesn't

PyKnowledge is built for students in Tawi-Tawi and across BARMM — designed around real classroom constraints, not ideal conditions.

What it is

Nine CHED-aligned modules with lessons, exercises, and quizzes — from your first print() to classes and object-oriented programming. Free, no ads.

Built in Tawi-Tawi

The curriculum, pacing, and offline-first design all come from teaching TRAC and BARMM students on shared computers with patchy island signal.

How to use it

Open the app, create a local profile (no email needed), and follow the path. Install it to your home screen once and everything works offline after that.

Where this curriculum is taught

Lesson content follows the CHED calendar and BARMM education standards used by schools across the Tawi-Tawi islands.

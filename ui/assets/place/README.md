## Note on quality
These were cropped from phone screenshots of the Google Photos detail view, so a couple still
have a sliver of the app's back-button in the top-left corner, and resolution is capped at what
the screenshot captured (720px wide, some originally as low as 480px tall before crop). For
`school-seal-logo.png` especially, swap in the original source graphic if you have it — a
screenshot recompression isn't sharp enough for a favicon/header mark at larger sizes.
